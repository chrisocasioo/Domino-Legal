1. Overview

Domino Punto Tracker ("the App") is developed by Santrico Apps. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how — and how little — we handle information in connection with the App.

The short version: we collect nothing. The longer version follows below.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any personal information or usage data. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any identifying information
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses
  • Game scores, history, or any content you enter
  • Location data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic logs sent to our servers
  • Analytics or usage statistics of any kind

3. No Accounts Required

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in with any service. You can download and use the App immediately without providing any personal information.

4. No Network Requests

The App makes no network requests of any kind. It does not connect to any server operated by Santrico Apps or any third party. There are no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics frameworks embedded in the App.

5. On-Device Storage

Any data generated by the App — such as game scores or settings — is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. This data is controlled entirely by you and can be deleted at any time by removing the App from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no data from any user, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

If our privacy practices ever change, we will update this policy and revise the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Apple App Store

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may collect certain information in connection with your download and use of the App in accordance with their own privacy policy. Please review Apple's Privacy Policy for details on data they may collect.
