About Domino Punto Tracker

Domino Punto Tracker is a simple, beautiful score-keeping app for domino games on iOS. Track points for two teams round by round, review the full match history, and set your own winning score — all without paper, pencil, or arguments. Built with Puerto Rico pride by Santrico Apps.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

BasicsHow do I start a new game?

From the main screen, tap the Reset / New Game button (usually a refresh or trash icon in the top corner). You'll be prompted to confirm before all scores are cleared, so there's no risk of accidentally wiping a game in progress.

BasicsHow do I reset the current game?

To reset and start fresh:

  1. Tap the New Game or Reset button at the top of the score screen.
  2. Confirm when prompted — this clears all scores and round history.
  3. Both team scores return to 0 and you're ready to go.
SettingsHow do I change the point limit?

Tap the progress bar at the top of the score screen. A picker or input will appear letting you choose your target score — common options include 100, 150, and 200, or you can enter a custom value. The progress bar will immediately update to reflect the new goal. You can change the limit at any point during a game.

ScoringHow do I undo a score entry?

Made a mistake? No problem. Tap the Undo button (↩ icon) right after entering a score to reverse the last entry. The score and round history will both revert to the previous state. Only the most recent entry can be undone at a time.

ScoringHow do I add points for a round?

Tap the + button under the team that won the round, then enter the number of points scored. Confirm to save the entry. The score card animates to show the new total, and the round is added to the history log below.

HistoryWhere can I see past rounds?

Scroll down on the main score screen to see the Round History section. Each entry shows the round number, which team scored, and how many points were added. This history is available for the current game only — it clears when you start a new game.

TechnicalDoes the app need an internet connection?

No. Domino Punto Tracker is fully offline. It makes no network requests and works anywhere — in your backyard, at the beach, or anywhere domino is played. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

TechnicalWill my game be saved if I close the app?

Yes. The app saves your current game automatically. If you close the app and reopen it later, your scores and round history will still be there exactly as you left them.
